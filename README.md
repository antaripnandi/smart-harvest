# Smart Harvest

**Smart Harvest** allows you to harvest and instantly replant mature crops with a simple right-click! No need to break and awkwardly replace crops ever again.

---

## Features

- **Right-Click to Harvest**: Right-click any fully mature crop to harvest its produce and automatically replant it at age 0.
- **Fair Economy & Zero Duplication**: Replanting requires a seed; 1 seed is automatically deducted from the harvested drops or player inventory. If no seeds are available, the crop breaks naturally without duplication.
- **Full Crop Support**: Works with Wheat, Carrots, Potatoes, Beetroots, Nether Wart, Cocoa Beans, and Torchflowers.
- **Fortune & Tool Enchantments**: Fully respects Fortune enchantments on whatever tool or item you hold.
- **Singleplayer & Server-Side**: Works on dedicated servers (vanilla clients can join without installing any mod) and in singleplayer worlds.
- **Zero Conflicts**: Clean Fabric event implementation with zero invasive mixins.

## Supported Crops

- **Wheat**: Drops wheat and bonus seeds; replants at age 0
- **Carrots**: Drops carrots; consumes 1 carrot to replant
- **Potatoes**: Drops potatoes; consumes 1 potato to replant
- **Beetroots**: Drops beetroots and bonus seeds; consumes 1 seed to replant
- **Nether Wart**: Drops nether wart; consumes 1 nether wart to replant
- **Cocoa Beans**: Drops cocoa beans; consumes 1 bean to replant
- **Torchflowers**: Drops flower; consumes 1 seed from inventory to replant if present
